# Art Direction Master Prompt

## Overview

Create a breathtaking cozy fantasy sanctuary that feels like a peaceful escape from the modern world.

The environment should evoke warmth, serenity, wonder and emotional comfort.

The world is handcrafted, timeless and untouched by civilization.

## Visual Style

- Premium stylized realism
- Painterly 3D illustration
- High-end animated film quality
- Disney/Pixar level environment detail
- Studio Ghibli inspired atmosphere
- Unreal Engine 5 quality lighting
- AAA game concept art
- Soft cinematic composition
- Cozy fantasy, Nature-focused, Dreamlike but believable

## Lighting

- Golden hour, Blue hour, Moonlight, Warm cabin lights
- Volumetric lighting, God rays, Soft shadows
- Ambient occlusion, Global illumination, Gentle bloom
- Realistic reflections, Dynamic weather lighting

## Environment

Ancient pine forest, Massive evergreen trees, Crystal-clear streams, Small waterfalls, Wooden bridges, Natural stone paths, Moss-covered rocks, Wildflowers, Fern fields, Tiny mushrooms, Flower patches, Glowing fireflies, Floating pollen particles, Morning mist, Gentle fog, Mountain valley, Calm lake, Natural springs, Hidden caves, Ancient giant trees, Wooden fences, Lanterns, Wind chimes, Wood piles, Cozy campfire, Small dock, Bird houses, Wooden benches, Tree stumps, Wild berry bushes, Animal trails.

## Living World

The environment constantly feels alive. Leaves slowly fall. Grass dances with the wind. Branches move naturally. Water gently ripples. Smoke curls from chimneys. Campfires softly crackle. Fireflies appear at night. Morning dew sparkles. Butterflies fly between flowers. Birds land on fences. Fish create tiny ripples. Dragonflies skim across the pond. Clouds slowly drift. Rain creates soft reflections. Snow quietly accumulates. Everything moves gently.

## Animals

Curious deer, Friendly foxes, White rabbits, Red squirrels, Owls, Small birds, Blue jays, Robins, Ducks, Swans, Hedgehogs, Raccoons, Butterflies, Dragonflies, Bees, Ladybugs, Frogs, Koi fish.

**No predators. No danger. No aggression.**

## Negative Prompts

No text, No logo, No watermark, No UI, No people, No city, No vehicles, No modern technology, No violence, No horror, No dark atmosphere, No clutter.

## World Philosophy

The sanctuary exists whether the player is present or not. Animals have their own daily routines. The weather changes naturally. Flowers bloom over time. Leaves accumulate in autumn. Snow slowly melts in spring. Fireflies only appear during warm nights. Rare magical events happen unexpectedly. Every visit feels slightly different. The world rewards observation rather than competition. The player never owns nature, they simply become part of it.

*Every image should make the viewer feel: "I wish I could live here."*
