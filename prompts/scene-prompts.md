# Scene Prompts

## Base Scene — Pocket Sanctuary

Create a cozy, peaceful fantasy forest sanctuary in a highly detailed painterly 3D illustration style.

A tiny wooden cabin sits beside a crystal clear pond surrounded by pine trees, moss-covered rocks, colorful wildflowers, mushrooms and soft glowing fireflies.

The atmosphere should feel warm, magical and comforting without looking childish.

Golden hour lighting with soft volumetric sunlight filtering through tall trees.

A small campfire gently burns in front of the cabin. Small wooden bridge. Flowing creek. Gentle waterfall. Wooden lanterns emitting warm orange light. Smoke slowly rising from the chimney. A deer quietly standing nearby. A fox sleeping near the fire. Ducks swimming in the pond. Butterflies floating through flowers. Birds occasionally flying overhead.

Everything should feel alive with subtle movement: Leaves drifting in the wind. Grass softly swaying. Water ripples. Fire crackling. Tiny glowing particles in the air.

No humans. The world should feel like a peaceful escape from reality.

Ultra detailed. Studio Ghibli inspired atmosphere mixed with realistic lighting. Pixar quality environment. Unreal Engine 5 quality. Highly detailed foliage. Soft cinematic depth of field. Warm color palette. Relaxing mood. Fantasy realism. 8K. Masterpiece. No text. No watermark. No UI.
